robo groove

» launch robo groove (free, no ads, no account)

why

I was hanging out with my 6-year-old daughter and she wanted us to play on ABCmouse, specifically to make rhythms together in its app called Bot Beats. But the loops weren't looping. There was a slight, ever so small gap between each loop.

As a seasoned FL Studio user, that just didn't sit well with me. Especially for my kid, who was learning what a loop is. I didn't want her learning from a janky app.

So I went to Claude and asked it to make a similar app. Then I thought: why not add a few more features? Like a teddy bear playing the drums, a fairy on the keyboard, a turtle on the bongos, a unicorn on the guitar and a fox on the flute.

Only the teddy bear on the drums was mine. My children asked for the rest. They also asked for the ability to save, and for other sounds, and I added those too.

So between an evening with the kids, playing and vibecoding with AI, we came up with Robo Groove together.

what it is

It's fun, it's easy to use, and it works right. It's not your full DAW experience with custom synth loops and LFO knobs, but kids don't need that.

They need to press a button, change the loop, overdub, and save their project without being pestered by ads or limitations, like some of us had to endure.

So take it. It's free. Enjoy it. If you have comments or suggestions, let me know below.

put it on your device

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the launch link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Robo Groove now sits on your home screen with its own icon and opens full screen, like any other app. No App Store needed.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the launch link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.

Windows / Mac: just use the launch link in any browser. Chrome and Edge also show an install icon in the address bar.

works offline

Open it once with internet and it saves itself on the device. After that it runs with no wifi: car, plane, grandma's house. Saved projects stay on the device too.

Two catches:

  • iPhone / iPad: the home screen icon keeps its own separate copy. After adding it, open it once from the icon while online. Offline from then on.
  • Updates: when the app gets updated, opening it online downloads the new version quietly in the background; it shows up the launch after that. Offline, it keeps the version it has.
