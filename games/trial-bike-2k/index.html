How to play

  • Grab the package, ride to the finish and deliver it. Win a star for finishing, one for no bonks and one for beating Dummy Dan.
  • Head or body on the ground is a bonk: you pop back at the last flag and the clock keeps going.

Controls

  • Green: gas. Red: brake. Yellow: hop. Blue: lean.
  • ↑ gas · ↓ brake · ← → lean · Space hop · R restart · Esc menu
  • Controller: RT gas, LT brake, stick lean, A hop, Y restart, Start menu.
