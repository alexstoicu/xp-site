about

who

The Nuked Duke.

By day I work with radiation. High stress, high stakes. Safety first, always. You still get zapped.

That's where "getting zapped" comes from. The perk: you get to joke about zapping the people in the lab who have zero idea what you're doing. They glow with confusion.

what this is

Stoic Science is the root of it all. That's all you need to know about that.

This place is the after-hours part. The unregulated part. No roadmap, no product line, no pre-drawn borders. Just free experimenting with AI and whatever comes out of it: games, apps, half-finished things, finished things, fallout.

why

For me. To learn, to grow, to see what happens.

The look is from the old web. The work is from right now. The eye is on a cyberpunk future. Past, present and future at once. Call it an atemporal existence. Time is just another half-life.

house rules

Plain, direct, no filter. Dry humor and bad puns, at high doses.

If that offends you, feel free to fuck off to a different plane of cyberexistence. You won't be missed.

Everyone else: be factual, be courteous, be direct, be childlike, be curious, be simple, be smart. We'll get along just fine.
